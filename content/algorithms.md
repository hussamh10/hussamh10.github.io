# Investigating the behaviors of social media algorithms.

Consider algorithms as cold machine agents in our information ecosystem. We have
handed them agency to filter, curate, and amplify information that aligns with
design and values baked into them through their developers' business models.

A lot of research documents the undesirable outcomes these systems produce at
scale. However, there is a lack of understanding where those outcomes come from,
i.e. from what specific behaviors do these pathologies emerge. Are they designed
into the algorithmic agents or do they emerge from them? The goal is to paint vivid
pictures of these algorithms, watch them closely, identify the patterns in their
behaviors, and diagnose the pathologies.
