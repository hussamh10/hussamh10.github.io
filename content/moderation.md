# Content moderation and what it cannot do.

Moderation: removing or constraining the users, content, and communities that
run against a platform's values, is one of the oldest interventions for making
social media *better*. It was also some of my earliest work: trying to make
moderation more effective, and to make it happen sooner, while coming to see how
poorly it sits inside the business model these platforms actually run on.

Since then I have come to think that content moderation cannot carry the weight
of being the intervention that fixes social media. It has its place and plenty
of genuine uses. But asking it to work against an entire engagement-based design
is asking far too much of it and not exploring more ambitious design changes.
For more on where the real leverage might be, stay tuned.
