I am a postdoctoral researcher in the CS2 @ UniGraz, Austria.

Previously I was a researcher at NYU, with Rachel Greenstadt at the PSAL lab, a PhD student at University of Iowa, with Rishab Nithyanand at the SPARTA lab, and a member of the CP3.

I research how social media platforms behave, especially their recommender systems. I measure how platforms have the capacity to change user behaviors and show what it would take to make them work better for the people using them.
